# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful and patient with people of every experience level,
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully,
- focusing on what is best for the project and the community,
- showing empathy toward other community members.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks,
- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention,
- publishing others' private information without their explicit permission,
- public or private harassment, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions,
- any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any channel the project runs) and when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take appropriate, fair action in response to behaviour they deem inappropriate. This can include editing or removing comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions, and temporarily or permanently banning a contributor.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on this repository (<https://github.com/flazo0/yeah/security/advisories/new>) and mark it as a conduct report, or contact a maintainer directly. All reports are handled confidentially.
